About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at Nanjing University, advised by Prof. Fengyuan Xu in the COSEC Group, led by Prof. Sheng Zhong. I expect to graduate in March 2027.

My research focuses on AI for Security (AI4Sec), combining large language models with program analysis to understand programs (e.g., smart contracts) and real-world (on-chain) attacks. I am particularly interested in AI agents for cybersecurity, tool-assisted vulnerability investigation, and blockchain security.

I am seeking academic and industry research positions, including postdoctoral opportunities, and welcome research collaborations. Please feel free to contact me.
